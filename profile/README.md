Retail Sols is an **Oracle Retail partner** building and running retail systems for
retailers across the **United States and the Middle East** — from merchandising and
inventory through to the point of service in the store.

We work at both ends of a retail estate: the Oracle Retail platform itself
(**MFCS**, **Xstore**, **Oracle Retail Cloud**), and the custom software around it —
store-side point-of-sale, payment integrations, peripheral/hardware bridges, and the
services that tie them to the rest of the business.

## What we build

| Area | What that means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Point of service** | Xstore implementation and extension, plus in-house POS backends — transactions, tenders, tills, receipts, and the lane-side services that drive printers, cash drawers and payment terminals. |
| **Merchandising & inventory** | Oracle Retail MFCS work: assortment, pricing, replenishment, and the data flows that keep stores and the home office in step. |
| **Integration & data** | Connecting retail ERP to payment gateways, third-party services and reporting, and making the resulting data usable. |
| **Cloud & migration** | Moving retailers onto Oracle Retail Cloud and newer releases, then keeping them there. |

## Engineering

The stack our teams actually work in:

- **Backend** — Java, Spring Boot, microservices, JDBC/SQL over Oracle and PostgreSQL
- **Front end** — React, Tailwind CSS, Rust/Tauri desktop clients
- **Retail platform** — Oracle Retail Xstore, MFCS, Oracle Retail Cloud, RICS integration
- **Store hardware** — JavaPOS/OPOS peripherals, payment terminals, receipt and slip printers
- **Quality** — POS test engineering across transactions, payments, peripherals and integrations
- **Data** — SQL, BI dashboards, retail sales and inventory analytics
